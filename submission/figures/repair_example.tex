\caption{Illustrative destruction and repair, not a benchmark outcome.
Squares are mandatory locations, circles are optional locations, and the
diamond is a demand. Filled visit nodes belong to the cycle; solid lines
are routing edges and dashed links show active coverage. Let
$p_a=p_b=p_c=1$, $p_u=2$, $p_v=3$, $P=5$, and $N_w=\{u,v\}$.
Removing $u$ preserves three mandatory visits but violates both requirements.
One feasible repair inserts $v$, restoring coverage and the prize quota.
The drawing does not assert that every randomized repair selects $v$ or
that an improvement is guaranteed.}
