# Introduction and discussion outline (partial manuscript)

## Introduction
- Define the routing/selection interaction directly from the PCCTP formulation.
- Prior RGRASP, MIP hybrid and MDM work: motivation for a strong recovered baseline,
  not a claim that generic hybridization is novel.
- Three questions: changed supports vs reordering; integer vs greedy repair;
  complete algorithm vs sufficiently developed MDM at charged total time.
- State present evidence and boundary: completed explorations, failed validation,
  ongoing longer-horizon work; no claim of novelty/new BKS or general superiority.

## Discussion
- Interpret same-start fixed-support replay as conditional component evidence.
- Add audited net support observations (23/27 changes, 20 quota witnesses,
  5 coverage witnesses), explicitly not reconstructed intermediate trajectories.
- Four TRAIN instances already covered by mandatory vertices: coverage rows
  redundant globally there; do not call their gains coverage-driven.
- MIP restricted neighborhoods can miss improvements and consume time; negative
  mean comparison retained, no unsupported attribution to model construction.
- Selected hardTRAIN, reused validation and reserved test; no significance claim.
- Stopping criteria/mining exposure, hardware and baseline fixes; no cross-machine
  speedup. Final contribution depends on the frozen, broader evidence still missing.
