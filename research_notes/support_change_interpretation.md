# Descriptive mechanism evidence from TRAIN supports

All27 corrected hybrid runs were checked using original scalar input. Final
visited support differs from the saved pre-repair support in23runs. Considering
the NET removed set without its final additions violates the prize quota in20
runs and coverage in5;20runs violate at least one. Thus coverage witnesses are
a subset of the prize-repair cases in this sample. These are net-change
feasibility witnesses, not recorded intermediate destruction trajectories.

Do not say that observed gains are predominantly driven by coverage: quota
restoration is the more frequent witness here. Four hardTRAIN geometries
already have all demands covered by mandatory visits. The same-start LKH
control supports the value of visit changes, but no current ablation isolates
the scoring formula, temporary infeasibility or temporary worsening alone.
A causal attribution to any one of those mechanisms needs further controls.

Source: diagnose_support_changes.py and support_change_diagnosis.json with
per-run removed/added IDs, prize slack and unique-cover witnesses. No reserved
validation/test outcomes are used in this descriptive analysis.
